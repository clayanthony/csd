# V5 — Expanded Semantic Worlds

## Delivered

- Five new landmark-rich map paintings, rendered as 3072×2048 scrolling worlds.
- A unique deterministic coordinate for every one of the 750 curriculum records.
- 18 semantic placement zones across home, land, time, community and meaning environments.
- Whole-word meaning matching so short strings cannot cause false placements (for example, `hat` no longer matches `that`).
- Literal associations such as fish at water, tree/leaf/log at forest edges, food at tables or gardens, books at school, tools at workshops and care words at care areas.
- Seeded neutral clearings for conversational and grammatical forms such as “thank you.”
- A golden compass that points toward the nearest unseen sign without revealing its word.
- Faster movement for the larger world and a landmark note on every word card.
- A fresh V5 save namespace so older map coordinates cannot corrupt the expanded-world save.
