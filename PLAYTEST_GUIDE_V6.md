# Supervised Learner Playtest Guide — V6

This build includes local learning statistics but does not transmit data. Use this guide with the learner’s informed consent and the project’s community/language approval process.

## Who to test with

- 5–8 beginning readers in the intended age range;
- 3–5 adult beginning Cree learners;
- at least 2 fluent Muscowpetung/Plains Cree speakers reviewing forms, meanings, images, locations and story context;
- a caregiver, teacher or facilitator present for every child session.

## Session shape

Keep the first session to 20–25 minutes. Ask the learner to complete one chapter: two six-word missions and the adaptive chapter check. Do not explain controls after the mission briefing unless the learner asks.

Observe without interrupting:

1. Can the learner begin and move within 30 seconds?
2. Do they understand the golden compass without verbal instruction?
3. Do they approach the companion or the sign first?
4. Can they connect at least four of six pictures with the English support?
5. Do they notice Cree before reading English?
6. Can they finish both missions without frustration?
7. Which words are missed twice or more?
8. Do rewards and friendship feel meaningful rather than distracting?

## Success thresholds

| Measure | V6 target |
|---|---:|
| Begin play without help | 80% of learners |
| Find the first sign within 45 seconds | 90% |
| Complete a six-word mission | 85% |
| First chapter-check accuracy | 70% or higher |
| Second exposure accuracy | improves by 15 percentage points |
| Voluntary continuation to another chapter | 60% |
| Reported frustration | below 2 on a 5-point scale |

## Local learner report

Open **MENU → LEARNER REPORT**. Record:

- words encountered;
- recognition accuracy;
- number of words queued for adaptive review;
- the displayed hardest words;
- trail leaves earned.

Do not record a child’s name in the game or attach personally identifying information to screenshots. The report remains in browser storage unless the user clears or restarts the journey.

## Language and cultural review

For each tested chapter, the fluent-speaker reviewer should mark every form, gloss, pictogram and environmental placement as **approved**, **revise**, or **remove**. Treat disagreement as a release blocker, not as learner error. No newly composed Cree sentence should be added until it is reviewed as a complete utterance.

## Decision rules after testing

- Replace any pictogram misunderstood by more than 25% of learners.
- Shorten a mission story if more than 20% skip or misread its task.
- Move a sign if more than 20% cannot connect its word with the nearby landmark.
- Add a word to earlier review when first-check accuracy is below 50%.
- Remove or defer any form that speakers consider dialectally unsuitable or too advanced for the intended introduction.
